### 🧠 Calmify – AI-Powered Mental Wellness Platform

Calmify is a comprehensive AI-powered mental wellness platform designed to help individuals monitor, understand, and improve their mental health through intelligent technology and personalized wellness solutions.

The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, mental health assessments, wellness resources, and community support into a single ecosystem, enabling users to track their emotional well-being and access appropriate guidance when needed.

#### ✨ Key Features

- 🤖 AI-based mood tracking and emotional analysis
- 📊 Mental health assessment and wellness checkups
- 🧘 Guided yoga postures and meditation support
- 🎵 Relaxing meditation and stress-relief music
- 🖐️ Smart wellness monitoring and stress detection
- 💬 AI chatbot for basic mental wellness assistance
- 👨‍⚕️ Psychologist and counselor discovery
- 📍 Location-based therapist and wellness center search
- 🤝 Community support and wellness meetups
- 🎮 Interactive games and activities for stress reduction
- 📚 Mental health awareness resources and educational content
- 📈 Personalized wellness recommendations
- 🔔 Progress tracking and self-improvement monitoring

#### 🎯 Problem Statement

Many individuals struggle to identify, monitor, and manage their mental health due to a lack of awareness, accessibility, and personalized support. Calmify aims to bridge this gap by providing a centralized platform that leverages AI and wellness technologies to promote mental well-being.

#### 🚀 Impact

- Encourages proactive mental health management
- Makes wellness resources more accessible
- Provides personalized recommendations through AI
- Connects users with professional support when needed
- Promotes a healthier and more balanced lifestyle
